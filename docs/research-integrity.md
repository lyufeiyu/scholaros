# Research Assistance and Academic Integrity

**English** | [简体中文](./zh-CN/research-integrity.md)

ScholarOS is a research assistance tool, not a researcher, author, or reviewer. It can organize materials, propose candidate questions and methods, record evidence, and draft Markdown, but it cannot replace academic judgment or responsibility.

## Responsibility by stage

| Stage | ScholarOS may assist with | Researchers must |
|---|---|---|
| Literature search | Compose queries, merge metadata, record source failures | Decide whether coverage is sufficient; read and verify original sources |
| Evidence organization | Create citation keys, summaries, and evidence-use notes | Confirm that evidence supports each claim; record counterevidence and limitations |
| Method design | Propose variables, baselines, metrics, and falsification conditions | Select the final design; address statistics, ethics, and domain validity |
| Writing | Produce structured research drafts and figure/table plans | Rewrite and verify facts and citations; ensure prose reflects the actual work |
| Results | Organize a reporting framework around uploaded materials | Guarantee data integrity; run analysis and interpret uncertainty |
| Authorship and submission | Provide checklists | Decide authorship, disclose AI use, and accept final responsibility |

AI should not be listed as an author. Whether generative AI may be used and how it must be disclosed depends on the current policies of the relevant institution, funder, journal, or conference.

## Nine deterministic checks

`PaperReviewer` runs the following checks on both the initial and revised draft:

1. Required section structure is present.
2. Traceable evidence exists.
3. In-text citations match the evidence ledger.
4. The method includes inclusion/exclusion rules, a primary metric, an analysis plan, and failure or stopping criteria.
5. Figure design notes are present and substantive.
6. Table design notes are present and substantive.
7. Result values can be located in uploaded result materials; without result materials, such values are rejected.
8. The draft states the scope of AI assistance and the researcher's final responsibility.
9. The body reaches a minimum depth for a complete argument.

These checks only detect predefined structural and consistency issues. Passing does not establish correct conclusions, appropriate statistics, ethical compliance, or publication readiness.

## Prohibited or inappropriate uses

- Submitting a generated draft without verifying its sources.
- Treating model-generated numbers, experiments, or citations as real results.
- Hiding AI use when disclosure is required by a course, institution, funder, or publisher.
- Using the system in place of ethics approval, informed consent, data governance, or authorship discussion.
- Sending unpublished papers, sensitive data, or restricted full text to a model or paper source that is not authorized to receive it.

## Before sharing or submission

- Verify central citations against the original text and confirm versions and licenses.
- Have researchers confirm the question, method, analysis code, data, and result interpretation.
- Address counterexamples, negative results, missing data, protocol deviations, and conflicts of interest.
- Complete required ethics review, author confirmation, AI disclosure, and data-availability statements.
- Remove placeholders and obtain review from a supervisor, collaborator, or relevant expert.
